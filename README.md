## 🚨 Problem

During disasters, response teams often face:

- Delayed identification of high-risk areas
- Unverified or duplicate SOS requests
- Difficulty tracking victims in real time
- Inefficient shelter allocation
- Lack of centralized rescue coordination
- Communication gaps between citizens and rescue teams

## 💡 Solution

SETU provides an integrated ecosystem for disaster intelligence and emergency response.
